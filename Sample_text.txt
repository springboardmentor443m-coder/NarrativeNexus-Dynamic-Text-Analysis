Just tried the new Meta Quest Ultra headset, and honestly, it's impressive. The immersive experience feels straight out of a sci-fi movie.  
However, several forums, including https://techcrunch.com, have raised concerns about user tracking and constant background data collection.

I went through dozens of community reviews. One person said, "Absolutely next-level graphics and responsiveness!!!"  
But another complained, "My device crashes every hour... it's just frustrating and overpriced."  
This kind of data is chaotic — full of random punctuation, symbols, and mixed sentiments.

User Feedback

"I regret buying this product. It feels rushed and buggy."
"The display quality is unmatched. A stunning leap forward in VR tech."
"It's powerful but complicated to set up. Needs better instructions."
"The software updates fixed a few issues, but performance is still inconsistent."

Some users mentioned long setup times, while others appreciated the quick pairing process.  
The overall sentiment seems polarized — strong positive and negative extremes, very few neutral opinions.

For analysis, words like "crashes", "buggy", and "regret" should fall under negative sentiment.  
Meanwhile, terms such as "unmatched", "stunning", and "impressive" should be classified as positive.  
Mentions of URLs, hashtags (#VR), or usernames (@metasupport) should be ignored during preprocessing.

This dataset is a good example of raw, unstructured feedback.  
Text normalization steps like lowercasing, punctuation cleaning, and stopword removal are crucial.  
Additionally, lemmatization can help unify variations such as "crashes" → "crash".
